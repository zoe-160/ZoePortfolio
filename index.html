02 · About

Designing across disciplines.

I earned a BFA in New Media from the University of Tampa, where I developed a multidisciplinary practice spanning digital design, UX, interactive media, storytelling, front-end development, and emerging AI tools.

My experience working with children with special needs has also shaped the way I think about clarity, accessibility, and creating experiences that work for different kinds of users—not just the ideal user.

My goal is to build thoughtful digital products that bring strong user experience, visual design, technology, and experimentation together.

I’m currently pursuing early-career opportunities in product design and UX where I can keep learning, contribute meaningfully, and work on products people genuinely use.
