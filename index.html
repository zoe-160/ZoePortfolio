02About + capabilities

Product design is where my creative background and interest in building things come together.

I like taking an idea and figuring out what it needs to become something people can actually use.

LinkedIn ↗

One of the things I valued most about studying New Media was being able to work across so many different areas. I earned my BFA from the University of Tampa with experience in digital design, UX, interactive media, storytelling, front-end development, and emerging AI tools.

My experience working with children with special needs has also shaped the way I think about clarity and accessibility. It made me more aware that a product cannot only work for the easiest or most obvious user.

Product design interests me because it gives those different skills a place to work together. Visual design, interaction, technology, and experimentation feel stronger to me when they are part of the same process instead of separate pieces of work.
