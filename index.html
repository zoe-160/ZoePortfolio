02About + capabilities

My background across design, media, and development informs how I approach product design.

I enjoy taking an idea from an early concept to an experience people can understand and use.

LinkedIn ↗

Studying New Media gave me the opportunity to work across digital design, UX, interactive media, storytelling, front-end development, and emerging AI tools. That range taught me to think about products from both a visual and functional perspective.

My experience working with children with special needs has also influenced how I think about clarity and accessibility. It made me more aware that an experience should account for different needs, levels of familiarity, and ways of interacting.

Product design gives those interests a place to work together. I am most engaged when visual design, interaction, technology, and experimentation support the same experience.
