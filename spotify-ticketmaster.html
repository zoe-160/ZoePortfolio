Spotify × Ticketmaster

01 · Problem

From listening to live.

Music discovery and concert discovery often happen across separate products and separate moments. This concept explores what it could feel like if the path from “I love this artist” to “I could actually see them live” felt more direct and context-aware.

The goal was not to redesign all of Spotify or Ticketmaster. It was to focus on one product opportunity and prototype the interaction quickly enough to evaluate the idea itself.

02 · Approach

AI-assisted, intentionally.

This project uses a different process from Scoops on purpose. Rather than creating traditional wireframes first, I used Figma Make as an AI-assisted prototyping tool to move from product idea to interactive concept quickly.

The rapid workflow let me spend more time evaluating flows, transitions, hierarchy, and feature behavior in context. The output is not presented as a substitute for UX thinking—it is the artifact of a faster exploratory process.
