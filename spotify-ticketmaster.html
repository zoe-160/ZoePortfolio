01

From listening to live

Music discovery and concert discovery often happen across separate products and separate moments. This concept asks what it could feel like if that transition were more direct and context-aware.

The goal was not to redesign Spotify or Ticketmaster. It was to isolate one opportunity and make it interactive quickly enough to judge the idea itself.

02

AI-assisted, intentionally

This process is intentionally different from Scoops. Instead of producing traditional wireframes first, I used Figma Make to move from product idea to interactive concept quickly.
