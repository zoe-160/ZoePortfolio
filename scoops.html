01 · Overview

The product

Scoops began as my New Media capstone at the University of Tampa and became a broader exploration of how digital products can encourage children to read, respond, create, and stay engaged.

The project gave me space to work across the full product experience—from structure and user flows through interface design, prototyping, a reusable UI system, and front-end implementation.
