01

The product

Scoops began as my New Media capstone at the University of Tampa and grew into a broader exploration of how digital products can encourage children to read, respond, create, and stay engaged.

The project let me work across the full experience: structure, user flows, interface design, prototyping, reusable components, and front-end implementation.

Case-study goal: show how decisions moved from messy early thinking into a coherent product system.
